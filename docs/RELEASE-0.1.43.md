# Windows beta 0.1.43

You can turn off automatic wallet locking in Settings > Updates. The setting is saved when you close the app. Automatic locking stays enabled until you change it.

When a wallet locks, the balance area says "Wallet locked" instead of showing zero. Mining continues, and you can stop it without reopening the wallet.

Sending may still ask for your password after the spending-key timeout expires. Turning off the screen timeout does not leave spending keys unlocked indefinitely.

The live-network target is December 1, 2026. This update does not reset the beta chain or your wallets. Test balances will not carry over to the live network.
