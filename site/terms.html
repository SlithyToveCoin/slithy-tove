2. Test coins and resets

The current downloads connect to a test network. Test SLTHY has no market value and must not be bought or sold. Test balances do not convert into live-network coins or give you a claim to future coins.

The test chain and wallets will reset before the live launch. Further resets may be needed during beta. A reset can remove test balances and transaction history from the active network. Old wallet files may remain on your computer and may not work with a replacement network.

November 1, 2026 is the target for the live launch. Testing may require that date to change.

3. Mining uses your computer

Mining runs on your computer's CPU. It can increase electricity use, heat and fan noise, and slow other programs. The local node also uses storage and internet bandwidth to download and verify the chain.

You are responsible for those costs and for checking that your computer has enough cooling and free disk space. Start with a lower power setting if you are unsure. Stop mining if the computer overheats or becomes unstable.

Mining does not guarantee a reward. Your results depend on your hardware, network difficulty and chance. Run the miner on equipment you own or have permission to use, in accordance with the rules of your workplace, hosting provider or network.

Never send wallet passwords, recovery words, private keys or wallet files to support. An official support reply will not ask for them.
