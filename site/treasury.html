01

What it can fund

The treasury is for children's books and reading programs. Related materials and costs must directly support that literacy work.

02

Who holds the wallet

At launch, CS Idea Labs LLC will control one encrypted offline treasury wallet. Its spending key will stay off this website and the public nodes.

03

How spending is reported

Each payment should have a public record showing the amount and transaction ID. The record should name the recipient and explain the literacy work funded, with supporting documents when available.

How community voting would work

I want people taking part in Slithy to have a vote on treasury spending. Voting is planned for later, once the network has regular activity. Proposals must support children's literacy.

Voting is not available yet. Before a vote can be binding, published rules must define who can vote and set turnout and approval thresholds. The rules must cover conflicts of interest and allow time to review proposals.

The people responsible for the treasury wallet would make payments for approved proposals. The voting rules must explain how those payments are signed.
