The literacy fund

01

What it can fund

Children's books and reading programs are the purpose of the treasury. Related materials and practical costs must support that literacy work.

02

Who holds the wallet

At launch, CS Idea Labs LLC will control one encrypted offline treasury wallet. Its spending key will stay off this website and the public nodes.

03

How spending is reported

Each payment should have a public record naming its recipient and purpose, with the amount and transaction ID. Supporting documents should be included when available.

How community voting would work

I want people taking part in Slithy to vote on how the fund is spent. That is planned for later, after the network has regular activity and a community taking part in it. Proposals must support children's literacy.

Voting is not available yet. Before a vote can be binding, the published rules need to explain who can vote, how many people must take part, and what approval requires. They also need to cover conflicts of interest, time to review a proposal, and how an approved payment gets signed.

Approved proposals would still need a payment from the people responsible for the treasury wallet. The voting rules will explain how that happens.
